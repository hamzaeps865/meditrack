# Pharmacy Connection — Pharmacist Role + Notifications + Patient Lookup + Dispense Slip

## Decisions confirmed
- **Separate `/pharmacy` route** for the pharmacist role (not sharing /admin)
- **Pharmacist scope:** dispense + view inventory + alerts only (no catalog CRUD, no stock management)
- **Low-stock alert:** when any batch drops below 10 units, auto-alert the pharmacist's notification bell

---

## G1 — Pharmacist Role (6th role, separate route)

**Role plumbing (7 files — same pattern as nurse):**
1. `users.ts` — add `'pharmacist'` to roleEnum

3. `rbac.ts` — add `| 'pharmacist'` to type Role

**New route tree:**
- `src/app/(dashboard)/pharmacy/page.tsx` — pharmacist dashboard: pending dispensings queue + low-stock alerts + patient search
- The pharmacist sidebar shows only: "Dispensing Queue", "Inventory", "Logout"

---

## G2 — Notifications (doctor → pharmacist → patient)

**Type extensions** in `notifications.actions.ts`:
- Add `'pharmacy'` and `'prescription'` to the NotificationItem type union
- Add icon + dotColor entries in `notification-bell.tsx`

**Pharmacist branch** in `getNotifications()`:
- Query pending dispensings (prescription items not yet dispensed from completed visits)
- Push notifications: "New prescription for [patient] — [medicine]"
- Also: low-stock alerts (batches below 10 units)

**Patient branch** in `getNotifications()`:
- Query the patient's recently dispensed prescriptions (within last 24h)
- Push: "Your prescription is ready — [medicine] × [quantity]. Please collect from the pharmacy."

---

## G3 — Patient Lookup at Pharmacy Counter

- A search box on the pharmacist dashboard: type patient name → filter pending dispensings by that patient
- Quick dispense flow: select batch + quantity → dispense → stock decrements → patient gets notified

---

## G4 — Dispense → Patient Notification

- In `dispensePrescriptionItem`, after dispensing: resolve the patient (via prescriptionItems → prescriptions → visits → patients) and the patient's user (via email join)
- The patient's next page load will show the notification in their bell

---

## G5 — Low-stock threshold at 10 units

- The pharmacist's notification bell shows alerts when any batch drops below 10 units
- Also visible as a prominent alert card on the pharmacist dashboard
