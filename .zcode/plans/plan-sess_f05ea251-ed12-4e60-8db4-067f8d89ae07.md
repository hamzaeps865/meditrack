# Complete Patient Journey Enhancement Plan — All 7 Steps

Each step below lists: what works today, what's missing, and the exact implementation.

---

## STEP 1 — Registration & Login (Before arriving)

**✅ Works:** Self-registration creates account + patient record, email login
**❌ Missing:**

### H1.1 — Phone Number Login (In-App OTP)
- **Schema:** Add `phone varchar(20)` to `users` + new `otp_codes` table (phone, code, expiresAt, used)
- **Actions:** `sendOtp(phone)` generates 4-digit code, stores with 5-min expiry, shows in toast (free — pluggable to SMS later); `verifyOtp(phone, code)` validates
- **Auth:** Branch in `authorize()` for phone+OTP vs email+password
- **UI:** "Email | Phone" toggle on login page → phone input → send code → 4-digit entry → verify
- **Migration:** users.phone + otp_codes table

### H1.2 — Urdu Language Support (Core Pages)
- **Install:** `next-intl` (free, MIT)
- **Approach:** Cookie-based locale (EN/UR) — no URL restructuring. `LanguageProvider` context in layout.tsx
- **Files:** `src/locales/en.json` + `src/locales/ur.json` with core strings
- **Translated:** login, register, sidebar nav, patient dashboard, appointments, prescriptions, reports, settings, common buttons
- **UI:** EN/اردو toggle button in sidebar + login page. RTL via `dir="rtl"` when Urdu active

### H1.3 — Profile Photo Upload
- **Schema:** Add `avatarUrl text` to `users` (stores base64 data URI — ~10KB for 128×128)
- **Upload:** Client-side `<input type="file">` → resize to 128×128 via canvas → base64 → server action saves to DB
- **Component:** Shared `Avatar` component (photo-or-initials) replaces 18 duplicated `getInitials` functions
- **UI:** Upload button in each role's Settings page. Photo shows in sidebar, top bars, patient/doctor cards

## STEP 2 — Booking Appointments

**✅ Works:** Online booking, family booking, doctor directory with ratings
**❌ Missing:**

### H2.1 — Patient Can Cancel Their Own Appointments
- **Action:** Add `patient` to the allowed roles in `updateAppointmentStatus` for cancellation only (patient can set status to `cancelled`, nothing else)
- **UI:** "Cancel" button on the patient's upcoming appointments (with a confirm dialog). Shows "Cancelled" badge after.
- **No reschedule** (too complex for now — they cancel + rebook)

### H2.2 — Hide Already-Booked Slots in Booking
- **Action:** In `booking-form.tsx`, query existing appointments for the selected doctor+date and filter out booked slots from the available grid
- **Result:** patient sees only truly available time slots

---

## STEP 3 — Arrival & Waiting

**✅ Works:** Check-in, nurse triage, doctor sees waiting list
**❌ Missing:**

### H3.1 — Patient Sees Their Queue Position
- **Action:** New `getPatientQueuePosition(patientId)` — counts how many checked-in patients are ahead of them today
- **UI:** On the patient appointments page, if status is `checked_in`, show a banner: "You are #3 in queue · Estimated wait: ~30 minutes"

---

## STEP 4 — Consultation

**✅ Works:** Doctor records diagnosis, vitals, prescriptions, lab orders, follow-ups
**❌ Missing:**

### H4.1 — Patient Sees Full Visit Record (diagnosis + notes)
- **Current:** Visit notes only show on prescription cards. A visit with no prescription is invisible.
- **Fix:** New `/patient/visits` page showing ALL visits (with or without prescriptions), each showing: date, doctor, chief complaint, diagnosis, vitals, notes, and linked prescriptions/lab orders
- **Sidebar:** Add "My Visits" nav item for patients

---

## STEP 5 — Payment

**✅ Works:** Auto-invoice on visit completion, admin billing page, printable receipt
**❌ Missing:**

### H5.1 — Patient Sees Their Invoices & Payment Status
- **Action:** New `getPatientInvoices(patientId)` — patient-scoped invoice list (reuses existing `invoices` table)
- **UI:** New `/patient/billing` page showing: outstanding balance, payment history, invoice status (pending/paid), printable receipt link
- **Sidebar:** Add "Billing" nav item for patients

---

## STEP 6 — Pharmacy

**✅ Works:** Pharmacist dispensing, stock management, notifications
**❌ Missing:**

### H6.1 — Patient Sees Medicine Cost & Pickup Status
- **Action:** `getPatientPrescriptionStatus(patientId)` — joins prescriptions → dispensings to show "Pending" vs "Dispensed ✓" + medicine unit price from catalog
- **UI:** Update the patient prescriptions page: each prescription shows dispense status + estimated cost

---

## STEP 7 — At Home (Follow-up & Self-Management)

**✅ Works:** Medication reminders, health report PDF, health score, loyalty, family, alerts, reviews
**❌ Missing:**

### H7.1 — Lab Test Results Visible to Patient
- **Action:** The `getLabOrdersForPatient` action already exists — just needs a UI
- **UI:** New `/patient/lab-results` page showing ordered tests, status (ordered/completed), and results when available. Sidebar nav item "Lab Results"

### H7.2 — Vitals Trend Chart
- **UI:** On the patient health report page (or a new `/patient/vitals` page), show a simple CSS/SVG line chart of BP/temp/weight over time from the visits table. No charting library — hand-built like the doctor analytics page.
